Good questions Frequently asked

The honest answers, in plain language.

Unbought is a prototype for a consent-based, currency-free mutual aid society. Nothing here processes payments, sends messages to other people, or uploads your information.

The basics

What is Unbought?

A practice of sharing labor, goods, care, education, and land without money changing hands between neighbors. The site is a local-first prototype: it shows what a shared commons could look like while every action stays in this browser.

What does “Built on Consent” mean?

Nothing is taken, assigned, or published about you without your agreement. Offers are invitations, requests need a clear yes, and your public profile shows only what you choose to share.

Is this a real service right now?

Not yet. There is no shared database, no real accounts, no payment processing, and no server transmission. You can explore the full experience locally; a future backend can use the same structured records.

Why the Poconos?

This first rollout is rooted in Pocono-area towns in Pennsylvania. The location examples across the site use places like Stroudsburg, East Stroudsburg, Mount Pocono, and Jim Thorpe. You can still enter your own location.

Membership and privacy

What does taking the pledge do?

It records your membership locally, along with the legal name and contact details the prototype needs to model real intake. It is not a legal contract and nothing is transmitted.

What does my public profile show?

Your chosen first name, the City/State you select, your skills, your commons totals, your availability, and a phone number only if you opt in. It never shows your legal name, street address, ZIP, housing status, or birthdate.

Why ask for a legal name and addresses at all?

The intake mirrors what a real mutual-aid trust would need for accountability and emergency contact. In this prototype it stays in your browser and is never shown on your public resume.

Can people under 18 join?

Not in this version. The sign-up form blocks birthdates that would make someone under 18. We are building a separate, safer path for younger neighbors before opening it up.

What are seed and founding members?

A seed member is the first person of a profession to sign up in a ZIP code. Founding members are the seed members who signed up during Unbought’s first year. Membership years begin at the exact moment of the vernal equinox, not on January 1. The first year runs from the March 20, 2026 equinox at 14:46 UTC to the March 20, 2027 equinox at 20:25 UTC — 4:25pm Eastern.

When do member labels appear?

The rollout target is the exact 2027 vernal equinox, March 20 at 4:25pm Eastern. Until then the dashboard shows labels as pending so nobody is labeled before the rollout moment.

Goods, labor, and exchanges

What can I offer on Free Stuff?

Legal, safe goods: groceries, household items, clothing, furniture, tech, tools, solar panels, raw materials, vehicles, gift cards from independent small businesses and co-ops, and other useful things. Vehicles are free; the recipient pays state registration and titling fees. Major-corporation gift cards, weapons, medication, recalled goods, and unsafe items are not allowed.

What can I offer on Free Labor?

Any safe skill you can freely share: repair, care, tutoring, organizing, design, and more. If materials are needed, say so. Regulated work requires an honesty check about licensing. The labor is never billed; any materials are agreed on separately at exact cost.

Can I add photos?

Yes. Members can attach up to three photos to a goods or labor post. They are resized in your browser and saved locally. Each photo has a storage-reference field for a future database, but nothing is uploaded now.

How do I find something nearby?

The Free Stuff catalog filters by category, condition, color, brand, city, state, and ZIP prefix. You can sort by newest, title, category, location, or nearest to a ZIP you enter. Active filters can be removed one at a time.

Can non-members get anything?

Non-members can record a local purchase intent today, and members will soon be able to opt in to set prices for goods and labor. The money would go to the Unbought fund; members always get things free.

Safety and accountability

How do safety reports work?

Reports are tied to the thing you were looking at — a listing, message, chat, profile, or dashboard item — so you never have to type someone’s name into a report form. You can attach local messages, pictures, or other files as evidence. Everything stays in this browser and nothing is transmitted.

Why are there no police reports?

This prototype does not contact authorities or any outside service. The review board is a local, consent-based model for thinking about accountability, not a legal process.

What happens during a reflection period?

Some full-access actions pause while a member works through the reflection and treatment steps recorded in their dashboard. The steps are local records in the prototype, with no outside review or reporting.

Will friends and groups be part of this?

Yes. Friends, groups, and social chat are being added next. Each group is capped at 150 people so conversation stays human-scale.

Prototype details

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local storage under the Unbought keys. Clearing site data or using the Demo Reset button removes it. There is no account recovery because there is no server account.

Does the site track me or show ads?

No. There are no analytics, ads, external frameworks, or third-party requests. The single catalog-style layout on Free Stuff is a visual nod only, built with Unbought’s own content and colors.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Use the suggestion form on the dashboard, or the waiting-list form on What’s to Come. Both record locally in this prototype and are ready to be connected to a real system later.
