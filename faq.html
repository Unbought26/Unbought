Good questions Frequently asked

The honest answers, in plain language.

Unbought is a prototype for a consent-based, currency-free mutual aid society. Nothing here processes payments, sends messages to other people, or uploads your information.

The basics

What is Unbought?

A practice of sharing labor, goods, care, education, and land without money changing hands between neighbors. The site is a local-first prototype: it shows what a shared commons could look like while every action stays in this browser.

What does “Built on Consent” mean?

Nothing is taken, assigned, or published about you without your agreement. Offers are invitations, requests need a clear yes, and your public profile shows only what you choose to share.

Is this a real service right now?

Not yet. There is no shared database, no real accounts, no payment processing, and no server transmission. You can explore the full experience locally; a future backend can use the same structured records.

How does site search work?

The search in the header is on every page. It looks through page copy, Free Labor and Free Stuff listings, current members, and saved groups in this browser. Results are local links; nothing is searched or sent on a server.

Why the Poconos?

This first rollout is rooted in Pocono-area towns in Pennsylvania. The location examples across the site use places like Stroudsburg, East Stroudsburg, Mount Pocono, and Jim Thorpe. You can still enter your own location.

Membership and privacy

What does taking the pledge do?

It records your membership locally, along with the legal name and contact details the prototype needs to model real intake. It is not a legal contract and nothing is transmitted.

What does my public profile show?

Your chosen first name, locally stored picture, tag line, bio, profession, the City/State you select, your skills, confirmed hours worked, items gifted, other commons totals, your availability, and a public phone number only if you opt in. It may show a pending or live seed/founder tag. It never shows your cell phone, legal name, street or mailing addresses, ZIP, housing status, birthdate, group, report count, amounts donated, comments, interactions, or member ID. Signed-in members can switch between the public resume and the private profile with View as, choose separate profile colors, and add friends from another member’s public profile.

Who can enter my work hours?

No one can add hours unilaterally. The worker and the receiver each choose the same agreed time from fixed options. Matching confirmations log that time; nothing is typed into an hours counter. If the choices differ, evidence goes to a review board and three randomly drawn active members choose one of the confirmed times; care circle members, students, observers, and either party cannot serve. Only those confirmed hours appear in your week, month, year, and all-time totals.

Why ask for a legal name and addresses at all?

The intake mirrors what a real mutual-aid trust would need for accountability and emergency contact. In this prototype it stays in your browser and is never shown on your public resume.

Can people under 18 join?

Full membership still opens at 18; the sign-up form blocks birthdates that would make someone under 18. Neighbors aged 15 and older can take part through the Academy's young-apprentice path instead: they learn free with Basic-Necessities-only access while enrolled, then complete one year of part-time work for Unbought after finishing. That work must be finished before their 25th birthday.

What can each membership track access?

Active Contributors take part in the full commons. Apprentices may use Basic Necessities only while enrolled. Care Circle members may use Basic Necessities with no labor obligation, and any limit is reviewable with evidence. Observers may read, explore, and donate. These boundaries are part of the Community Guidelines and are enforced locally in this prototype.

What are Basic Necessities, and who decides how mutual aid works?

Basic Necessities are Food, Health Care, Dental, Mental Health, Shelter, Clothing, Gas Cards, Uber Gift cards, spot on the waitlist for land, access to the Academy. No luxury items or nice to haves. Groups can decide how mutual aid will work within these limits.

What is an alliance?

Alliances are other groups within the Unbought network. Groups decide whether to accept an alliance between them, and working members vote on alliance terms and guidelines.

What are seed and founding members?

A seed member is the first person of a profession to sign up in a ZIP code. Founding members are the seed members who signed up during Unbought’s first year. Membership years begin at the exact moment of the vernal equinox, not on January 1. The first year runs from the March 20, 2026 equinox at 14:46 UTC to the March 20, 2027 equinox at 20:25 UTC — 4:25pm Eastern.

When do member labels appear?

The rollout target is the exact 2027 vernal equinox, March 20 at 4:25pm Eastern. Until then the dashboard shows labels as pending so nobody is labeled before the rollout moment.

Is there a celebration planned?

We hope to partner with an organization and throw a huge festival somewhere in the United States on March 20, 2028, one full year after the 2027 rollout, celebrating the movement and its seed and founding members. The moment matters because we will have been fully rolled out for a full year, meaning something must be working. It is an intention, not a booked event: no venue, partner, or ticket exists, and the festival page is a planning page only.

Do seed members get a thank-you message?

Yes, in the plan. A live system would email seed members automatically, thanking them for starting the movement and suggesting ways to help — from talking to other professionals and posting on social media to scouting donated housing, starting a donation-revenue Unbought business, donating, volunteering services, and offering skills. In this prototype your dashboard shows the draft locally; no email is sent and nothing is transmitted.

Goods, labor, and exchanges

What can I offer on Free Stuff?

Legal, safe goods: groceries, household items, clothing, furniture, tech, tools, solar panels, raw materials, vehicles, gift cards from independent small businesses and co-ops, and other useful things. Vehicles are free; the recipient pays state registration and titling fees. Major-corporation gift cards, weapons, medication, recalled goods, and unsafe items are not allowed.

What can I offer on Free Labor?

Any safe skill you can freely share: repair, care, tutoring, organizing, design, and more. If materials are needed, say so. Regulated work requires an honesty check about licensing. The labor is never billed; any materials are agreed on separately at exact cost.

What if my job title is not listed?

Choose “Write my own title” when posting. In a live site that title would go to the site maintainer so the list can grow. In this prototype it is saved locally in Dashboard → Suggestions with the title, pillar, listing, and location; nothing is transmitted.

Can I add photos?

Yes. Members can attach up to three photos to a goods or labor post. They are resized in your browser and saved locally. Each photo has a storage-reference field for a future database, but nothing is uploaded now.

How do I find something nearby?

Free Labor can be searched by title, skill, neighbor, or dollar amount, then filtered by pillar, that pillar’s service titles, online or in-person format, donation price band, city, state, and ZIP prefix. Free Stuff filters by category, condition, color, brand, donation price band, city, state, and ZIP prefix. Both directories can be sorted by newest, title, location, or nearest to a ZIP you enter, and active filters can be removed one at a time.

Can non-members get anything?

Providers can opt in to a fixed suggested donation price on their goods or labor listings. The price is shown on the card and in the listing details, and both directories let you filter and search by it. Guests see that price and cannot change it; every cent is recorded locally for the Unbought donation fund. Members always get the same things free through mutual consent, and no real payment is processed. Only pledged members can post a service or product; guests can browse and donate but cannot open the posting form.

What can members see in the fund?

Only signed-in members see their own hours worked and amounts donated by week, month, year, and all time, plus the current Unbought donation fund balance and their share of it. The ledger also records donations, academy tuition, non-member purchases, and confirmed hours. Your legal name, addresses, ZIP, birthdate, group, and report count stay out of the public resume.

The Academy

Does the Academy cost anything?

It is free for pledged members: no tuition, fees, or debt. People who have not taken the pledge may enroll at the standard non-member tuition set by the guild. Every cent goes to the Unbought donation fund. That tuition is a future policy only; this prototype does not enroll anyone or process a payment. The Academy is Phase 2, so its Dashboard link is hidden in this Phase 1 build.

Can someone under 18 take Academy courses?

Yes. Neighbors aged 15 and older who are not already working in the commons can learn free by promising to work for Unbought part time — 1–15 hours a week — for one year upon completing the program. The service year must be finished before their 25th birthday. Full membership itself still opens at 18.

What access do students have while enrolled?

Students are in a limited standing: Basic Necessities only while they are in the program. That is a safety boundary, not a punishment, and it is reviewed under the Community Guidelines.

What if the program or the service year isn't finished?

Not completing the program creates a tuition obligation to the Unbought donation fund. The promised year of part-time work must be completed before the 25th birthday, or the same tuition offered to non-members applies. It is a community commitment reviewed under the Community Guidelines, not a legal contract or a debt collected by this prototype.

Safety and accountability

How do safety reports work?

Reports are tied to the thing you were looking at — a listing, a comment on a listing, a message, community or social chat, a profile, or a dashboard item — so you never have to type someone’s name into a report form. You can attach local messages, pictures, or other files as evidence. Everything stays in this browser and nothing is transmitted.

Who is allowed to vote in a review board case?

Involved parties, friends of involved parties, Observers, and Care Circle members do not vote, so the decision stays impartial. They can still attach evidence and take part in the review board conversation. Voting is open to pledged members whose physical address is in the same county as an involved party. If one member is in State A and the reported member is in State B, members in both respective counties may vote. Members of the same group may also vote unless they have a report against them from the last year, and members of allied groups may vote unless the group voted to close its review board to alliances outside its own ZIP code; alliances within that ZIP code are always included. Working members vote on alliance terms and guidelines. County eligibility uses your physical address; counties and street addresses stay private and never appear on a public resume.

Why are there no police reports?

This prototype does not contact authorities or any outside service. The review board is a local, consent-based model for thinking about accountability, not a legal process.

What happens during a reflection period?

Some full-access actions pause while a member works through the reflection and treatment steps recorded in their dashboard. The steps are local records in the prototype, with no outside review or reporting.

Will friends and groups be part of this?

Yes. Dashboard → Friends & Groups automatically places you in the main town group, such as Stroudsburg Group I. When a town group reaches 150 people, another Stroudsburg group opens with a Roman numeral — II, III, … XI — and neighbors can choose which numbered group to join. The first member of a new numbered group is not a seed member. The town group profile shows the city, state, and ZIP below its name. Every other group is a social or support circle you make or join, and group names link to their own group profile.

Seven admin seats rotate weekly for workers only, staggered across one-to-seven-week cycles. Care Circle, Observer, and Apprentice members cannot be admins. Current admins can add another admin, cancel a group, delete group chat posts and replies, require an impartial review, change the group’s community guidelines, and start a guideline vote in chat. You can leave a non-town group when another admin exists; only an admin can cancel a group. Reports made by a temporary admin need evidence, go to an impartial review, and are never decided as criminal matters by member vote. Admin votes on guideline changes are recorded as Agreed, Not agreed, or More discussion needed.

Search current members by chosen name, profession, or town and send a local friend request; public profiles have the same Add friend action. You can keep one group of your own and write social chat drafts with exactly Audience, Title, Message, and picture. There is one local account, so no invitations are sent and no one else’s account changes. Friend lists and groups are capped at 150 people.

Can a group create a business or a brand?

In Phase 2, yes. A group can create a Business and gather a collection of related services and products into a Group Brand. That is how groups create industry inside a moneyless society. Pledged members still receive the group’s labor and goods free through mutual consent; there is no price charged to members. This rolls out on the vernal equinox, March 20, 2027 at 4:25pm Eastern, alongside the rest of the full site, and the controls stay hidden in this Phase 1 build.

Only a current group admin can open Group settings. There, admins set the group’s physical location or mark it Online if it has no physical location. They can also record optional co-op legal information — forming a co-op is a legal step outside Unbought, and nothing is filed or transmitted here. The Unbought Brand is optional: if a group chooses it, the Unbought legal guild and administrative guild will work with you to ensure safety. Every group setting stays in this browser.

What do dashboard notifications show?

Local drafts — messages, requests, comments, group posts, social chat drafts, and a one-time notice when a review board case opens and you are eligible to vote. If you are involved, a friend of someone involved, an Observer, or in the Care Circle, you will not get a vote notice, but you can still add evidence and join the conversation. Each notice is one whole link: clicking anywhere on the item marks it read and opens the target. Listing comments stay with their post on Free Labor or Free Stuff. In a live system, neighbors would be notified about messages, requests, and comments on their posts. Nothing is delivered to another person or device from this prototype.

Can I talk with neighbors on a listing?

Yes. Each Free Labor and Free Stuff post has a comment thread with replies, and every comment has its own report button. The whole card is a link to the details dialog, and an opened card can be restored with a ?listing= local link. Group profiles are separate: they have Members, Admins, Group chat, Offers in Free Stuff & Free Labor, and Group details. Group chat replies stay attached to the post they answer; there are no separate group comments or Group involvement block, and listing comments are not copied into a group.

Where are the community guidelines and consequences?

They are on the Community Guidelines page. They include the Ten Consent Commandments, monthly pledge review, the care circle, access limits, therapy and proof of treatment, community review, majority votes, exile, and the reflection period before reapplication.

Prototype details

Where is my data stored?

In your browser’s local storage under the Unbought keys. Clearing site data or using the Demo Reset button removes it. There is no account recovery because there is no server account.

Does the site track me or show ads?

No. There are no analytics, ads, tracking scripts, or external frameworks. The only third-party embed is the privacy-enhanced Alan Watts video on the pledge page. The single catalog-style layout on Free Stuff is a visual nod only, built with Unbought’s own content and colors.

How do I report a bug or suggest something?

Use the suggestion form on the dashboard, or the waiting-list form on What’s to Come. Both record locally in this prototype and are ready to be connected to a real system later.
