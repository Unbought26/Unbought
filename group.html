Group profiles show the local member list, admins, group chat, and the Free Stuff and Free Labor posts connected to the group. Everything here is stored in this browser; nothing is sent or published to another device.

Overview The Commons Profile Settings

In a live system, group membership and posts would come from the shared commons. This prototype keeps them local, counts people from the group’s member list, and sends no invitations or updates.
