Labor & Product Safety Standards

These are the current standards for keeping labor and goods exchanges clear, honest, and safe. Members read and acknowledge them when they sign the pledge. Posting, editing, and requesting also require an acknowledgment of this version.

What the acknowledgment proves. Unbought records that you read these standards on this device, with the date and the standards version. It is not legal advice, it does not replace the Community Guidelines, and it is not a promise of legal protection. The record is not sent to Supabase. You can review it in Dashboard → Settings and download a copy there.

Free labor standards

  1. Labor is never charged.

    Time, skill, and care are offered without a fee. There is no sliding scale, tip jar, wage, invoice, barter score, or debt created between neighbors. Unbought labor is free, not discounted.

  2. Only exact material costs may be repaid.

    If a repair or build needs supplies that are not available free on Unbought, the person requesting the work pays the exact receipt price. The worker does not front the cost, and Unbought adds no markup or handling fee.

  3. Consent sets the scope.

    Both people agree in plain language on what will happen, how long it will take, where it will happen, and what is out of bounds. Anyone can change their mind or stop at any time, and the answer is accepted without argument.

  4. Regulated work is labeled honestly.

    Medical, dental, mental-health, and legal offers must state whether they are General Peer Education or a Licensed Clinical/Legal Service delivered by someone with active licensure. Never imply a qualification you do not hold.

  5. Privacy and emergency limits stay clear.

    Unbought does not collect or store medical records or diagnoses. Peer support is not emergency care. Call 911 for an emergency or 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline. Nothing here asks anyone to stop using emergency services.

Full detail is on the Free Labor Standard and in the safety and scope guardrails.

Product safety standards

  1. Goods are free for pledged members.

    Pledged members share and receive goods through mutual aid in the commons. No proof of need, income check, intake form, or explanation is required. Dignity is not a screening criterion.

  2. Items must be safe and legal to share.

    Items must be clean, usable, within date, and free of recalls. Food must be properly stored. Weapons, medication, recalled products, and anything unsafe to use are not listed. When in doubt, ask a peer host before posting.

  3. Describe goods honestly.

    State the real condition of the item and any limits on its use. Every exchange starts with a clear description and ongoing mutual consent. Either person can decline or stop at any time.

  4. No real payment is processed.

    No payment is processed in the beta.

Full detail is on the Goods Policy and in the goods safety guardrails.

Safety reports and boundaries

Safety concerns can be reported from the thing you were viewing. Report text, evidence, reasons, and reporter identity always stay in this browser. With optional private sync on, the only report-related data sent is the reported member’s local ID plus an open/settled status so private-data access for the reported web developer can pause.

This is a local-first community model, not a legal process. Unbought does not contact authorities, carry out enforcement, or decide criminal matters by member vote. The Community Guidelines explain current rules and consequences, and the FAQ explains how safety reports and review boards work.

Acknowledging these standards is a promise to follow them, not a claim that any exchange is risk-free. Use ordinary care, respect local law, and stop any exchange that feels unsafe or unclear.
