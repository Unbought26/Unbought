The goods policy

Shared freely in the commons.

Pledged members receive goods through mutual aid without prices. In this prototype, people who do not take the pledge may record a purchase intent for a good or service; no real payment is processed and no money changes hands.

Goods are free

Pledged members share and receive goods through mutual aid in the commons. People who do not take the pledge may record a purchase intent for goods in this prototype; no real payment is processed and the amount is tracked locally as a planned intent for the Unbought donation fund.

Materials belong to the work

If a repair or build needs supplies that are not available free on Unbought, the person requesting the work pays their exact receipt price. That cost is for materials, never for labor or for goods on this page.

No proof of need

No income check, no intake form, and no explanation required. Dignity is not a screening criterion. Take what helps your household.

Safe and legal to share

Items are clean and in usable condition. Food is within date, recalls are checked, and weapons, medication, and unsafe goods are not listed.

Pledged members do not pay for goods here. Non-members can record a local purchase intent in this prototype, with the amount tracked as a planned intent for the Unbought donation fund; no real payment is processed.

Goods safety guardrails

  • Items must be clean, usable, and shared in good faith. Food must be within date, properly stored, and free of recalls.
  • We do not list weapons, medication, recalled products, or anything unsafe to use. When in doubt, ask a peer host before posting.
  • Every exchange starts with a clear description and ongoing mutual consent. Either person can decline or stop at any time.
