Community standards Guidelines & consequences

The consent commandments.

Unbought is not held together by money or management. It is held together by consent, honesty, and neighbors willing to answer for what they do. These guidelines apply to every member, in every space of the commons.

Prototype boundary. This is a local-first model of community accountability. No report text, evidence, reason, or reporter identity is sent to a server, no authority is contacted, and no enforcement action is carried out. Cases, reviews, and votes are recorded only in this browser. If optional private sync is on, the only report-related data sent is the reported member's local ID plus an open/settled status to pause a reported web-developer admin's access.

The Ten Consent Commandments

  1. Nothing without consent.

    An offer is an invitation, never a claim on anyone. A request needs a clear yes. Silence, hesitation, or a yes given under pressure is not consent. Anyone may change their mind or withdraw at any time, and the answer is accepted without argument.

  2. No money changes hands between neighbors.

    Labor and goods are free. Only exact, receipt-backed material costs may change hands when supplies cannot be found free, and a vehicle recipient pays state registration and titling fees to the state, not to Unbought. No wages, tips, barter scores, payouts, or cryptocurrency.

  3. Tell the truth about what you offer.

    Describe the real condition of goods, the real scope of a skill, and any materials needed. Regulated work must be labeled honestly as general peer education or as licensed clinical or legal service with an active license. Never imply a qualification you do not hold.

  4. Private information stays private.

    A member's legal name, street address, ZIP, housing status, birthdate, and cell phone are never published. The public resume shows only a chosen first name, picture, tag line, bio, profession, member-selected City/State, skills, commons totals, availability, and a public phone number if that member opts in. Do not repeat or infer another person's private details.

  5. Keep every exchange safe.

    No weapons, medication, recalled goods, unsafe items, or gift cards for major corporations. Agree on scope, timing, and access needs before showing up. Respect scent-free rooms, chairs, captions, and boundaries. Safety reports are local, tied to the post or message, and never sent to police or any outside service in this prototype. Report text and evidence stay in this browser; with optional private sync on, only a minimal open/settled flag for the reported admin is sent.

  6. Keep the commons safe for minors.

    No porn and no prostitution. There are free websites that can serve you outside of Unbought. Anything else unsuitable for minors must carry an NSFW tag, and that tag never overrides this rule.

  7. No extraction, no leverage.

    Do not use someone's need against them. No guilt, pressure, unpaid obligation, grooming, favor banking, or tracking another member's every move. Mutual aid is not a transaction and care is not a debt.

  8. Every person has worth without work.

    Participation is not a productivity score. A neighbor who cannot work this month is still a neighbor. Tell the truth when capacity changes so the community can respond with care instead of punishment.

  9. Be honest in the prototype.

    There are no real accounts, payments, messages to other people, or uploads to a server. Say plainly when an action is only recorded locally. Do not present a local note, intent, or vote as if it reached someone else or has legal force.

  10. Accept review and protect the commons.

    If you are reported or a guideline is broken, community members review the facts. Attach relevant local evidence when you have it, respond in your own words, and accept the outcome without retaliating against a reporter, reviewer, or voter. Retaliation is its own violation. Invite people in, share skills, and leave the space safer than you found it. When a limit or revocation is needed, use the reflection period to come back right, if and when the community allows it.

Basic Necessities

Basic Necessities are Food, Health Care, Dental, Mental Health, Shelter, Clothing, Gas Cards, and Uber Gift cards. No luxury items or nice to haves. Groups can decide how mutual aid will work within these limits.

Alliances

Alliances are other groups within the Unbought network. Groups decide whether to accept an alliance between them, and working members vote on alliance terms and guidelines.

Group admin reading duty

Group admins are responsible for upholding these Unbought Community Guidelines and for maintaining their own group’s community guidelines. When someone is notified of an admin seat, the role stays pending until they read both sets of guidelines and accept. If they do not accept by 6pm the day before the term starts, or they say they do not want the seat, a backup admin is chosen. No one can act as an active admin without that reading and acceptance.

Consequences and care, step by step

Nothing here is automatic. Each step begins with a community review of the facts, and the goal is always to understand what happened before deciding what access makes sense.

Monthly pledge review

Each month is checked after it ends. If you do not complete your pledged hours, your pledge status is frozen. Service requests and shared-content changes pause until those hours are completed; you can still offer labor and read the documents. Hours confirmed after the freeze count toward the shortfall.

If the pledge stays frozen for more than 30 days, you become an Observer. To re-pledge, three impartial active members must approve it. Observers, Care Circle members, apprentices, students, frozen members, and the applicant cannot serve on that review, and the local ballots stay anonymous. Care Circle members with no labor obligation are not frozen for missed hours.

What changed?

Before any limit is set, community members review what happened. A recent tragedy, moving, loss of ability to work, illness, caregiving, or another reason may mean you belong in the Care Circle instead of being treated as noncompliant.

The care circle

In the Care Circle you start with Basic Necessities. If you confirm locally that you cannot meet the pledge, full access can be turned on without saving a diagnosis, document, medical record, or note. Nothing is uploaded; only the resulting access status and time may be kept.

Guideline or law violation

If you violate a community guideline or a law, your account is limited to Basic Necessities while community members review the case. This is a safety boundary, not a declaration of guilt by the prototype.

Repeated offenses and exile

Repeated offenses can result in “exile” — revocation of membership. Community members review every case, and a revocation requires a majority vote, not a decision by one person.

Reflection and reapplication

If your access is revoked, you enter a reflection period whose length is decided by the community. Only after that period can you apply again. Your application goes immediately to review and a vote; the majority that decides is made up of members within your area.

Who votes on a review board case

To keep the process impartial and connected to the issue, the involved parties, friends of the involved parties, Observers, and Care Circle members do not vote on the case. They are still encouraged to provide evidence and join the conversation in the review board. Voting is completely anonymous: ballots store no name, member ID, or link back to the voter, and results show only the choice, reason, and date. Group guideline votes, hour-review votes, and three-member re-pledge reviews are anonymous in the same way.

Voting is open to pledged members whose physical address is in the same county as an involved party. If one member is in State A and the reported member is in State B, members in both respective counties may vote. County eligibility is based on your physical address, not your mailing address. Members of the same group may also vote unless they have a report against them from the last year. Members of allied groups may vote as well, unless the group voted to close its review board to alliances outside its own ZIP code; alliances within that ZIP code are always included. Working members of groups and alliances vote on alliance terms and guidelines. Counties and street addresses stay private and never appear on a public resume; nothing is transmitted.

Repair, therapy, and proof of treatment

Therapy is encouraged for anyone who has violated a guideline. In some cases the community may ask for confirmation that recommended reflection or treatment was completed before full access returns. This is about repair and safety, not shame. No diagnosis, document, medical record, therapy note, or treatment detail is saved or uploaded in this prototype. Any change is recorded only as a resulting access status and time.

What each standing means

Active

Full participation: post goods and labor, request exchanges, chat, and help review cases.

Inactive · observe only

Requesting and shared-content changes pause while the pledge is frozen. You can still offer labor and read the documents. After more than 30 days frozen you become an Observer, and requesting resumes only after three active members approve your re-pledge.

Care circle

Basic Necessities only at first. Full access can be confirmed locally without storing proof details.

Limited

Basic necessities only while a guideline or law violation is under community review.

Student

Basic Necessities only while in a learning track. Future learning plans are listed only on What’s to Come. Nothing here enrolls anyone or charges money.

Exiled

Membership revoked by majority vote, followed by a community-decided reflection period before reapplication.
